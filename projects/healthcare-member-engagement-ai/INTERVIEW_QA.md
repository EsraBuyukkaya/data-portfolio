# Interview Q&A

## How would you explain this project?

I built a healthcare product analytics prototype for a mobile engagement platform scenario. The project uses synthetic member data, SQL, Python, and Streamlit to identify at-risk members and recommend outreach actions. I focused on privacy-safe data, practical product metrics, and clear stakeholder communication.

## Why did you use synthetic data?

Real healthcare member data can include protected health information. I used synthetic data so I could demonstrate the workflow safely without using private patient or member records.

## Where is SQL used?

SQL is used to join member, event, and outreach tables; calculate product metrics; compare outreach channels; and identify members who need action.

## What makes this an AI/product innovation project?

The project turns user behavior into an intelligent workflow. It does not just report numbers. It identifies risk patterns and recommends the next best action for a product or operations team.

## Is this a production AI system?

No. It is a portfolio prototype. A production version would need real data governance, HIPAA review, security controls, monitoring, user testing, and clinical/legal review where appropriate.

## What would you say you personally learned?

I learned how to translate a job description into a project: identify the requested skills, design a realistic scenario, build a data model, write SQL analytics, and create a dashboard that supports business decisions.

## How could this help a product team?

It gives the team a shared view of member engagement, renewal risk, outreach effectiveness, and recommended actions. That helps product and operations teams prioritize what to build or improve next.
