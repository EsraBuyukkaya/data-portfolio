SAMPLE SERVICE AGREEMENT

This sample agreement is fictional and is included only for portfolio demonstration purposes.

1. Parties
This Service Agreement is entered into by Northstar Analytics LLC, a fictional consulting company, and Brightlane Retail Inc., a fictional client company. The provider will deliver data reporting and workflow automation services to the client.

2. Services
The provider will design monthly reporting dashboards, document workflow requirements, and provide reasonable support during the implementation period. The client will provide timely access to relevant non-confidential business information needed to complete the services.

3. Confidentiality
Each party agrees to protect confidential information received from the other party. Confidential information may include business plans, pricing, customer lists, technical materials, and internal process documents. The receiving party may not disclose confidential information to third parties except as required to perform the services or as required by law.

4. Term and Termination
This agreement begins on January 1, 2026 and continues for six months unless terminated earlier. Either party may terminate this agreement with thirty days' written notice. The client may terminate immediately if the provider materially breaches this agreement and does not cure the breach within ten business days after receiving written notice.

5. Payment
The client will pay the provider a fixed monthly fee of $2,000. Invoices are due within fifteen days of receipt. Late payments may pause work until the account is brought current.

6. Indemnification
The provider agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the client from third-party claims arising from the provider's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of applicable law. The client agrees to indemnify the provider from claims arising from materials, instructions, or data supplied by the client.

7. Governing Law
This agreement will be governed by and interpreted under the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of law principles.

8. Entire Agreement
This agreement represents the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements related to the services.
