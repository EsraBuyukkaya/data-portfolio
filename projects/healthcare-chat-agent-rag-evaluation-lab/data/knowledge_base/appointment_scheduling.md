# Appointment Scheduling

Patients may reschedule through scheduling by contacting the office or using the patient portal. Same-day cancellations should be routed to the scheduling team when clinic policy requires staff review. The assistant may explain the next step, but it should not promise appointment availability.

Common approved wording: "You can reschedule through scheduling, and a staff member can confirm the available times."

Common approved wording: "You can cancel through scheduling, and the team can confirm whether any policy applies."
