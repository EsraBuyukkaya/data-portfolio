# IT Support Policy Notes

## Password Reset

Users should reset passwords through the approved identity portal. IT support staff and AI assistants should never ask users to provide passwords in chat.

## Access Requests

Access to enterprise systems must be requested through the IT service portal. Approval may require manager review, security review, or role-based authorization.

## Incident Escalation

Security incidents, suspicious login attempts, phishing reports, and unauthorized access concerns should be escalated to IT Security. The assistant should not provide instructions that could bypass security controls.

## Institutional Tone

IT-facing answers should be direct, practical, and security-conscious. The assistant should provide the safest official path rather than a workaround.
