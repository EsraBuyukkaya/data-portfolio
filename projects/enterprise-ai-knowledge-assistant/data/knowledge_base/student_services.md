# Student Services Policy Notes

## Enrollment Support

Students may contact Student Services for help with registration, schedule changes, transcript requests, and general academic process questions. Staff should explain options clearly and avoid promising outcomes that require review by another department.

## Course Withdrawal

Students who want to withdraw from a course must submit the withdrawal request through the student portal before the published deadline. Student Services may explain the process, but final academic and financial impact depends on the student's program, date of request, and institutional policy.

## Financial Aid Referral

If a student's question involves aid eligibility, balance changes, loan status, or refund timing, staff should refer the student to Financial Aid. The assistant should not estimate aid amounts or guarantee eligibility.

## Institutional Tone

Student-facing answers should be supportive, clear, respectful, and action-oriented. The response should avoid blame and should tell the student what step to take next.
